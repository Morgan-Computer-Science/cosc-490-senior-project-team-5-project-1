Morgan State University Computer Science Advising Knowledge Base

Degree Requirements
The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science requires 120 total credits.
COSC 490 Senior Project is taken during the senior year.
Students should follow the official curriculum sequence when planning courses.

Freshman Year
Freshman year includes COSC 111 Intro to Computer Science I.
Freshman year includes COSC 112 Introduction to Computer Science II.
Freshman year includes MATH 241 Calculus I.
Freshman year includes MATH 242 Calculus II.

Sophomore Year
Sophomore year includes COSC 220 Data Structures and Algorithms.
Sophomore year includes COSC 241 Computer Systems and Digital Logic.
Sophomore year includes COSC 281 Discrete Structure.
Sophomore year includes MATH 312 Linear Algebra I.

Junior Year
Junior year includes COSC 349 Computer Networks.
Junior year includes COSC 351 Cybersecurity.
Junior year includes COSC 352 Organization of Programming Languages.
Junior year includes COSC 354 Operating Systems.
Junior year includes MATH 331 Applied Probability and Statistics.

Senior Year
Senior year includes COSC 458 Software Engineering.
Senior year includes COSC 459 Database Design.
Senior year includes COSC 490 Senior Project.

Prerequisites and Advising
COSC 112 is taken after COSC 111.
Students should verify prerequisites and course sequencing with the department or an academic advisor.
Students should meet with an academic advisor for course planning and degree progress questions.

Student Support Resources
Tutoring and academic support resources are available for students.
Students can contact the Computer Science department for advising guidance.
Internships and career support are important for Computer Science students, especially before senior year.
